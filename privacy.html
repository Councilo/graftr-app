Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Vendaru (vendaru.com), a peer-to-peer courier marketplace. We are committed to protecting your personal information under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018. Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@vendaru.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, and a password — stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash, never in plain text, so we cannot see or recover your actual password.
  • Job data: the pickup and drop-off addresses you enter, your chosen time window, and the price calculated from them. Addresses are matched to a fixed table of UK town and city centres to calculate distance — we do not use GPS or track your location.
  • Proof photos: a courier uploads a photo at pickup and at delivery for each job. These are stored with our hosting provider and reachable only by the link generated for that job — the link isn't guessable, but isn't access-restricted either, so treat it like any other unlisted link.
  • Technical data: standard web request data (IP address, browser type) logged by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account (a session token is stored in your browser's local storage after you log in).
  • To calculate delivery quotes and connect a customer's job with a courier.
  • To show each side of a job who they're dealing with — a customer sees the name of the courier who accepted their job.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Vendaru runs on Vercel's infrastructure: our database (Vercel Postgres) and proof photos (Vercel Blob) are hosted there. We do not run advertising and do not use advertising cookies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. A customer and the courier assigned to their job can see each other's name as part of completing that job; neither can see any other account's data.

5. Your Data Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email privacy@vendaru.com.

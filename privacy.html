Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Welcome to Vendaru (vendaru.com). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@vendaru.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on Vendaru, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our services, or otherwise contact us.

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and location preferences when creating an account.
  • Transaction & Booking Data: Details of services requested, store orders placed, delivery addresses, and communications with verified UK businesses.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and interaction metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our platform for legitimate business purposes:

  • To facilitate account creation, authentication, and secure login.
  • To connect customers with local UK verified businesses, trade professionals, and local grocery stores.
  • To process bookings, inquiries, and orders accurately.
  • To improve platform usability, search relevancy, and service quality.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce platform safety.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

Vendaru uses Google AdSense to serve advertising content when you visit our website. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared with verified UK service providers when explicitly requested by you to fulfill a booking or order inquiry.

5. Your Data Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by Vendaru. To exercise these rights, email privacy@vendaru.com.

Prohibited Items

DRAFT — pending review by the business owner and a UK solicitor. Not yet legal advice.

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-19

To keep couriers, customers and parcels safe, some things can't be sent through Vendaru. This list is general information, not legal advice.

On this page

  1. Never send these
  2. Size, weight and value limits
  3. Couriers may refuse a parcel
  4. If a rule is broken

1. Never send these

You must not post a job for any of the following, and couriers must not carry them.

  • Weapons: firearms, ammunition, replica or imitation firearms, and other weapons.
  • Dangerous goods: explosives, fireworks, flammable or corrosive liquids and gases, other hazardous goods, and damaged batteries.
  • Illegal drugs and other controlled substances.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes and other age-restricted goods. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: whether an age-check process will be added; until then these are not accepted]
  • Cash and high-value items: money, jewellery, bank or payment cards, cheques, identity documents and passports.
  • Live animals and human remains.
  • Stolen or counterfeit goods.
  • Indecent or obscene material.
  • Anything that is illegal to send, carry or possess.
  • Perishable goods that need temperature control (for example fresh food or medicines that must be kept cold), unless this has been agreed in advance.

2. Size, weight and value limits

Vendaru couriers are individuals in ordinary vehicles, so parcels must be small enough to handle safely. Our proposed limits:

  • Size: the parcel must be carryable by one person in a normal car.
  • Weight: up to 30 kg.
  • Declared value: up to £500, unless agreed in advance.

[PROPOSAL, OWNER TO CONFIRM: parcel size, weight and value limits]

Anything above the limits is not accepted.

3. Couriers may refuse a parcel

A courier may refuse to take, or to carry on with, any parcel they reasonably suspect breaks these rules or is unsafe. Couriers do not open parcels, so please describe the contents honestly. We don't check what is inside a parcel, so you are responsible for what you send.

4. If a rule is broken

If we find, or are told, that a prohibited item has been sent, we may cancel the job, suspend the account, and tell the police or another authority where the law requires or where it is needed to keep someone safe. If you are not sure whether something is allowed, ask us through Help before you post. If you think you have seen a prohibited item, tell us there straight away.

Emergency? If someone is in danger, call 999. Vendaru can't respond to emergencies.

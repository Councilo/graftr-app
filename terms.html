7. How a job works

  1. Open. The job is posted and shown to couriers registered with Vendaru, who see the area, not your exact addresses. We can't promise that a courier will accept it, or how quickly.
  2. Accepted. A courier accepts. They then see the full addresses and you can chat.
  3. Collected. The courier takes a photo of the parcel at pickup.
  4. Delivered. The courier takes a photo at delivery.

A job can also be cancelled. If a courier hands a job back before collecting it, it returns to Open for another courier. Jobs are not matched by distance and there is no promise that a courier will be nearby.

Proof photos may show people or places. They are stored as described in the Privacy Policy.

8. Cancelling

You can cancel a job in the app before a courier has accepted it, for a full refund (or nothing charged, if you have not paid). Once a courier has accepted it, the Cancel button is locked because cancelling then incurs a fee; you can ask support to cancel it and the fee is applied. Once the parcel has been collected, different rules apply. They are set out in full in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Your legal rights are not affected. Nothing in these Terms or in our in-app cancellation and refund rules takes away the rights you have under consumer law. Our rules are in addition to them.

1. Your legal rights come first

This policy is in addition to your legal rights and never reduces them. In particular, if you are a consumer:

  • Consumer Rights Act 2015. Services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill. If a service isn't, you can ask for it to be repeated or for a price reduction.
  • Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. For a service you buy online you normally have 14 days from the day the contract is made to cancel without giving a reason. You lose that right once the service has been fully carried out. If you ask for the service to start straight away, for example by choosing pickup "Now", and then cancel, you must pay for what has already been provided.

3. Asking for a refund

Sign in and choose the order, then use Report a problem or the refund request option. Pick the closest reason:

  • Damaged
  • Not delivered
  • Courier didn't turn up (no-show)
  • Wrong price
  • Other

Describe what happened in as much detail as you can. The proof photos and the order chat are taken into account. You can see the status of each request in the app:

  • Pending: waiting for us to look at it.
  • Approved: refunded in full.
  • Partially approved: refunded in part. We say how much and why.
  • Denied: not refunded. We give a written reason.
  • Auto-approved: approved automatically because a rule applied (for example, you cancelled before a courier accepted).

An administrator can approve a request in full or in part, or deny it with a written reason. You can also ask for help through Help, for example if you can't sign in.

4. How refunds are paid

Payment is currently tracked in the app and settled by hand, so refunds are also settled by hand by Vendaru. A job's payment status shows as unpaid, paid, partially refunded, refunded or void.

  • If you haven't paid yet: nothing is charged for a job that is cancelled or refunded in full.

5. If a courier lets you down

If a courier cancels, hands the job back or doesn't collect the parcel, it goes back on the marketplace and another courier can accept it. You aren't charged again. If in the end no one delivers it, or the parcel is lost, you get a full refund. If a courier's behaviour worries you, see "Report a problem" or Help.

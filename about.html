About Vendaru

Vendaru is the United Kingdom's premier local services directory and instant shopping marketplace. Our mission is to connect consumers directly with verified UK small businesses, certified trade professionals, local shops, and essential service providers across every major UK town and city.

Our Platform Features

🛠️ 100+ Verified UK Services

From accredited plumbers and electricians to estate agents, car repair, beauty salons, tutors, and cleaners.

🛒 On-Demand Local Groceries

Order fresh groceries, household items, and daily essentials from local UK stores delivered right to your door.

📍 City Location Filtering

Tailor your searches across London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Bristol, and 30+ UK cities.

⭐ Transparent Pricing & Reviews

Compare real business pricing, direct website links, official phone numbers, and genuine customer feedback.

Empowering UK Local Businesses

Vendaru provides independent UK companies with high-visibility digital storefronts, enabling local trade professionals and shop owners to reach nearby customers, publish service offerings, and receive direct quote requests without middleman fees.

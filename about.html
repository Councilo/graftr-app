About Vendaru

Vendaru is a peer-to-peer courier marketplace covering the UK. A customer posts where a parcel needs to go, gets an instant price based on the real distance between the two towns named, and a courier accepts the job and carries it from pickup to drop-off.

How it works

📍 Post a job

Enter a pickup address, a drop-off address, and a time window. The price is calculated from the real distance, not guessed.

🚴 A courier accepts

Couriers browse open jobs and accept the ones they can do — no bidding, no middleman markup.

📷 Proof at both ends

The courier photographs the parcel at pickup and again at drop-off, so both sides can see it actually happened.

🗺️ Live status

Every job moves through Open → Accepted → Collected → Delivered, visible to the customer the whole way through.

Two kinds of account

Register as a customer to send parcels, or as a courier to earn by delivering them. Pricing is calculated from a fixed table of UK towns and cities matched against the address text — an address that doesn't name a recognised place is rejected rather than priced from a guess.

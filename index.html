Send a parcel with an independent courier

Vendaru is a peer-to-peer courier marketplace covering the UK. Tell us where a parcel needs to go and see the price before you post it, based on the real road distance. An independent courier accepts the job, collects the parcel and delivers it, with a photo taken at pickup and another at drop-off so you can see it actually happened.

How it works

  1. Post a pickup address, a drop-off address and a pickup time (or “now”). The price is £3.50 plus £1.37 per road mile, with a minimum of £5.00, and it is fixed once you post.
  2. A courier registered with Vendaru accepts the job.
  3. They photograph the parcel at pickup, then again at drop-off.
  4. Follow the job's status the whole way through and, while it is on its way, the courier's position on the map.

Two accounts, one platform

Register as a customer to send parcels, or as a courier to earn by delivering them. Couriers are independent people who choose which jobs to accept; Vendaru provides the platform that connects you. There is no card payment yet: payment is settled manually and the instructions are shown in the app.

Vendaru

Send a parcel with a courier near you

Vendaru is a peer-to-peer courier marketplace covering the UK. Tell us where a parcel needs to go, get an instant price based on the real distance, and a courier picks it up and delivers it — with a photo taken at pickup and another at drop-off, so you can see it actually happened.

How it works

  1. Post a pickup address, a drop-off address, and a time window.
  2. Get a quote — a courier accepts the job.
  3. They photograph the parcel at pickup, then again at drop-off.
  4. Track the job's status the whole way through.

Two accounts, one platform

Register as a customer to send parcels, or as a courier to earn by delivering them. Every job is priced from the real straight-line distance between the two UK towns named in the addresses.
